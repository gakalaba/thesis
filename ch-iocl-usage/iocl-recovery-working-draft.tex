\section{Recovery Protocol and Proof}

\noindent\textbf{Status.} A concrete candidate for analysis, not a proved
failover protocol. This listing covers recovery only. It adds no normal-path
replication round, no pre-round-1 POQ reservation, and no requirement that normal
final ACKs wait for round 2. Repeated-failure safety and reconstructed-POQ safety
remain proof obligations. No recovery-specific durable decision record is assumed.

\paragraph{Inputs and notation.}
At shard $i$, $C_i$ is the ordered log selected by the underlying view-change
protocol, $U_i$ its selected unordered bag, and $W_i=U_i\setminus C_i$ the pending
owned operations. Log selection must preserve the underlying replication
protocol's guarantees; arbitrary union of conflicting logs is not assumed.
Handles include owner identity. $N$ counts cluster shards; $p,P,m,S$ count
predecessor operations, predecessor shards, successor operations, and successor
shards. Historical evidence includes local request entries, predecessor-arrival
arrays, valid retained buffers, and retained compaction metadata. Rejected stale
messages do not become evidence merely by being buffered.

For every relevant handle $o$, keep $b[o]=(state,t,origin,\pi)$, with states
$\Hole,\Proposed,\Accepted,\Resolved$. Only resolved bindings are usable.
The origin is historical or agreed replacement. Use symmetric sets
\[
 \mathcal R=\{o:b[o].state=\Resolved\},\qquad
 \mathcal U=\{o:b[o].state\ne\Resolved\},\qquad
 \mathcal K=\{o\in\mathcal R:b[o].origin=\Historical\}.
\]
Known is the historical subset of Resolved. Proposed and Accepted remain
Unresolved. Foreign handles represent dependency information and never enter
local POQs. Initial response sets $V_o,H_o,N_o$ contain distinct respondents
returning VALUE, HOLE, and NOPE, respectively. Inspect local evidence separately.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\textbf{Recovery term} & \textbf{Message} & \textbf{2PC analogy}\\\hline
Owner & -- & Coordinator\\
Hole respondent & -- & Participant\\
Candidate & $TENTATIVE(o,\pi,t)$ & Prepare\\
Acceptance & $ACCEPT(o,\pi,t)$ & Yes vote\\
Historical counter-evidence & $COUNTER(o,\pi,t_{old})$ & Reject with evidence\\
Resolution & $RESOLVE(o,\pi,t,kind)$ & Decision/commit\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
This is a mapping of concepts, not an invocation of a complete crash-safe 2PC
implementation. Rejecting a candidate does not abort the client operation.

\paragraph{Attempts and views.}
Every cross-shard envelope identifies its sender and installed view.
An inquiry names the requester's recovery attempt $r=(i,v_i,\eta)$; a proposal
has identifier $\pi=(\owner(o),v_{\owner(o)},\eta,k)$. Responses name the
inquiry/proposal they answer. Match values and identities as well as identifiers.
Replies and votes are counted by distinct current sender incarnation.
All handlers serialize with local normal-protocol transitions.

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{View filtering and dispatch}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{Receive}{$j,v,m$}
  \If{$knownView[j]\ne\bot\land v<knownView[j]$}
    \State \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \If{$knownView[j]=\bot\lor v>knownView[j]$}
    \State $knownView[j]\gets v$
    \State Invalidate unfinished reply/vote counts from older incarnations of $j$
    \State Requery live collections or resend the current proposal as needed
    \State Invalidate uncommitted proposals owned by $j$ in older views
    \State Retain resolved bindings and their historical evidence
  \EndIf
  \If{$m$ is a recovery control message}
    \State Dispatch the matching handler, even while recovering
  \ElsIf{$mode=\Recovering$}
    \State Buffer $m$ without READY, append, ACK release, or execution
  \Else
    \State Invoke the original normal handler
  \EndIf
\EndProcedure
\Statex Higher-view messages update the map and are processed normally by type.
\Statex Unknown views are not zero. Own view allocation uses the base view-change
\Statex protocol; incrementing an arbitrary stale replica counter is insufficient.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Install state and begin collection}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{BeginRecovery}{}
  \State $mode\gets\Recovering$; $initialDone\gets\mathsf{false}$
  \State Disable normal effects and POQ draining
  \State Install a new own view $v_i$; $r\gets(i,v_i,\Call{FreshId}{})$
  \State Reset volatile reply counts, proposals, subscriptions, and remote-view knowledge
  \State Install selected $U_i,C_i$, snapshot, and retained metadata
  \State Extract historical arrival bindings; mark them resolved with historical origin
  \State $W_i\gets U_i\setminus C_i$
  \State Register missing owned bindings and predecessor bindings as holes
  \For{each key $K$}
    \State $\lready[K]\gets\max(\{0\}\cup\{e.\fin+1:e\in C_i,e.K=K\})$
  \EndFor
  \State $shardTS\gets\max(\{0\}\cup\{e.\fin+1:e\in C_i\})$
  \For{each shard $j\ne i$}
    \State \textbf{send} $SEARCH(r,W_i)$ to $j$
  \EndFor
  \For{each distinct predecessor $q$ whose binding is needed}
    \State \textbf{send} $LOOKUP(r,q)$ to $\owner(q)$, or handle locally
    \State Report locally held historical evidence for $q$ to its owner
  \EndFor
  \State Await every initial SEARCH and LOOKUP reply; HOLE counts as an initial reply
\EndProcedure
\Statex Inquiries establish view fences. Answers wait for local state installation,
\Statex never for the responder's global recovery. Log scanning can run in parallel
\Statex with collection. No cluster-wide quorum substitutes for contacting every shard.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Immediate local answers and subscriptions}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{LocalAnswer}{$o$}
  \If{a resolved binding or retained historical value for $a(o)$ exists}
    \State \Return $VALUE(o,t)$
  \ElsIf{$mode=\Recovering$ and this recovery is seeking exactly $a(o)$}
    \State \Return $HOLE(o)$
  \Else
    \State \Return $NOPE(o)$
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\Statex
\Procedure{OnSearch}{$j,r_j,W$}
  \State Apply the view fence and ensure selected local state is installed
  \For{each $o\in W$}
    \State $ans[o]\gets\Call{LocalAnswer}{o}$
    \If{$ans[o]=HOLE(o)$}
      \State Register $(j,r_j)$ as waiting for evidence/resolution about $o$
    \EndIf
  \EndFor
  \State \textbf{send} $SEARCHREPLY(r_j,ans)$ to $j$
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{OnLookup}{$j,r_j,o$}
  \State \textbf{assert} $\owner(o)=i$; ensure selected local state is installed
  \State $ans\gets\Call{LocalAnswer}{o}$
  \If{$ans=HOLE(o)$}
    \State Add $(j,r_j)$ to $waiters[o]$
  \EndIf
  \State \textbf{send} $LOOKUPREPLY(r_j,ans)$ to $j$
\EndProcedure
\Statex Proposed/accepted candidates never generate VALUE replies.
\Statex SEARCH requests owned bindings only; predecessor LOOKUPs go to their owners.
\Statex Evidence found independently at a nonowner is routed to the owner.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Initial collection and candidate selection}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{OnInitialReply}{$j,r_j,answers$}
  \If{$r_j\ne r$}
    \State \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \State Count the matching inquiry reply from the current sender incarnation
  \State Classify owned-operation answers into $V_o,H_o,N_o$
  \State Invoke HistoricalValue for owned VALUE replies; assert resolved-value agreement
  \State Owner LOOKUP replies resolve corresponding dependency bindings
  \If{all initial inquiry replies have arrived and $\neg initialDone$}
    \State $initialDone\gets\mathsf{true}$
    \For{each $o\in W_i$}
      \If{$o\in\mathcal R$}
        \State \Call{NotifyWaiters}{$o$}
      \Else
        \State $t\gets\max(shardTS,\lready[o.K])$; $shardTS\gets t+1$
        \State $\pi\gets(i,v_i,r.\eta,\Call{NextProposal}{o})$
        \State $b[o]\gets(\Proposed,t,\Replacement,\pi)$
        \State $participants[o]\gets H_o$
        \State \textbf{send} $TENTATIVE(o,\pi,t)$ to all participants
        \State \Call{TryResolve}{$o$}
      \EndIf
    \EndFor
    \State Process buffered TENTATIVE messages
  \EndIf
\EndProcedure
\Statex Initial response sets are historical classifications, not permanent binding states.
\Statex Preserve recovered values exactly; do not raise them to replacement floors.
\Statex Predecessor arrivals affect finalTS through the original ordered fold;
\Statex normal arrivalTS need not exceed predecessor arrivals.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Historical evidence and owner-mediated dissemination}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{HistoricalValue}{$o,t$}
  \If{$o\in\mathcal R$}
    \State \textbf{assert} $b[o].t=t$; \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \State Cancel any uncommitted conflicting candidate
  \State $b[o]\gets(\Resolved,t,\Historical,\bot)$
  \If{$\owner(o)=i$}
    \State \textbf{send} historical $RESOLVE$ to every prior proposal recipient
    \State Use the applicable current owner attempt/proposal identifiers
    \State \Call{NotifyWaiters}{$o$}
  \Else
    \State \textbf{send} $EVIDENCE(o,t,\text{owner attempt})$ to $\owner(o)$
  \EndIf
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{NotifyWaiters}{$o$}
  \State \textbf{assert} $\owner(o)=i\land o\in\mathcal R$
  \For{each live subscription $(j,r_j)$ for $o$}
    \State \textbf{send} $OWNER\_VALUE(o,b[o].t,r_j)$ to $j$
  \EndFor
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{OnEvidence}{$j,o,t,r_j$}
  \State \textbf{assert} $\owner(o)=i$; match the current owner collection/attempt
  \State \Call{HistoricalValue}{$o,t$}
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{OnOwnerValue}{$j,o,t,r_j$}
  \State \textbf{assert} $j=\owner(o)$; match a live local lookup subscription
  \If{$o\in\mathcal R$}
    \State \textbf{assert} $b[o].t=t$
  \Else
    \State Discard an uncommitted candidate and record the owner's resolved binding
  \EndIf
\EndProcedure
\Statex Resolving a historical HOLE also requires notification; discovering a value
\Statex locally is not sufficient to unblock its requesters. Nonowners send evidence
\Statex to the owner rather than broadcasting competing decisions.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Participant handling of a tentative candidate}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{OnTentative}{$j,o,\pi,t$}
  \State \textbf{assert} $j=\owner(o)\ne i$; reject obsolete owner attempts/proposals
  \If{$\neg initialDone$}
    \State Buffer until initial collection completes; \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \If{$o\in\mathcal R$}
    \State \textbf{send} $COUNTER(o,\pi,b[o].t)$ to $j$; \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \State A duplicate acceptance must name the same proposal and candidate
  \State $b[o]\gets(\Accepted,t,\Replacement,\pi)$
  \State \textbf{send} $ACCEPT(o,\pi,t)$ to $j$
  \State Keep the binding unresolved and unusable; further inquiries receive HOLE
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{OnCounter}{$j,o,\pi,t_{old}$}
  \State Match the current proposal or corresponding historical evidence inquiry
  \State \Call{HistoricalValue}{$o,t_{old}$}
\EndProcedure
\Statex If historical evidence is discovered after ACCEPT but before RESOLVE,
\Statex send COUNTER immediately. Proving that such evidence cannot invalidate
\Statex a racing owner decision is a safety obligation of this candidate protocol.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Owner decision and resolution receipt}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{OnAccept}{$j,o,\pi,t$}
  \If{$b[o].state\ne\Proposed$ or proposal/value does not match}
    \State \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \State Record acceptance by $j$'s current incarnation
  \State \Call{TryResolve}{$o$}
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{TryResolve}{$o$}
  \If{$b[o].state\ne\Proposed$}
    \State \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \If{some participant has not accepted this exact proposal}
    \State \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \State $(t,\pi)\gets(b[o].t,b[o].\pi)$
  \State $b[o]\gets(\Resolved,t,\Replacement,\pi)$
  \State \textbf{send} $RESOLVE(o,\pi,t,\Replacement)$ to every participant
  \State \Call{NotifyWaiters}{$o$}
\EndProcedure
\Statex
\Procedure{OnResolve}{$j,o,\pi,t,kind$}
  \State \textbf{assert} $j=\owner(o)$; match a current owner attempt
  \If{$o\in\mathcal R$}
    \State \textbf{assert} $b[o].t=t$; \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \If{$kind=\Replacement$}
    \State Require matching ACCEPTED proposal; otherwise obtain current owner resolution
  \EndIf
  \State Historical resolution may override an uncommitted candidate
  \State $b[o]\gets(\Resolved,t,kind,\pi)$
\EndProcedure
\Statex Current OWNER\_VALUE notifications also resolve matching lookup subscriptions.
\Statex Conflicting resolved values are invariant violations, not overwrite cases.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[p]
\caption{Candidate POQ reconstruction and resumption}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{TryRebuild}{}
  \If{$\neg initialDone$ or some required binding remains unresolved}
    \State \textbf{return}
  \EndIf
  \State Preserve $C_i$ and all stored timestamps and predecessor evidence
  \State Clear pending POQs; keep draining disabled
  \For{each owned $o\in W_i$}
    \State $e.\arr\gets b[o].t$; $e.\fin\gets e.\arr$
    \State Fill $e.A$ in original predecessor order from resolved bindings
    \State $e.ready\gets\mathsf{false}$; insert $e$ into $POQ[e.K]$
  \EndFor
  \For{each pending $e$}
    \State Remove $e$ before changing its sort key
    \State $e.\fin\gets\max(e.\arr,\Call{OriginalFold}{e.A})$
    \State $e.ready\gets\mathsf{true}$
    \State $\lready[e.K]\gets\max(\lready[e.K],e.\fin+1)$
    \State Reinsert by $(e.\fin,e.\rho)$
  \EndFor
  \State Reset unknown predecessor-ACK receipts; rebuild per-key ACK wait queues
  \State Re-register dependencies and request predecessor final ACKs
  \State Enable normal POQ head draining and original round-2 replication
  \State Process buffered operational messages through view filtering
  \State Recompute ACK eligibility with the original same-key prefix condition
  \State Restore/replay from a consistent snapshot and applied prefix
  \State Deduplicate requests and preserve/reconstruct prior response values
  \State Resume original execution barriers and replies; $mode\gets\Normal$
\EndProcedure
\Statex The reconstruction is the proposed algorithm, not an assumed POQSafe oracle.
\Statex Its prefix and historical-ACK compatibility are to be tested and proved.
\Statex POQs on distinct shards need not be equal or globally sorted together.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Failures during recovery.}
Restart from selected replicated state after another failover. Surviving resolved
bindings return VALUE; proposed or accepted-but-unresolved bindings return HOLE
when being recovered. A newer owner view invalidates old uncommitted proposals,
not retained resolved evidence. Obsolete replies cannot satisfy new collections.
A learned newer participant incarnation invalidates its old unfinished vote.
Owners that already resolved a binding preserve it and answer new lookups with it.
Candidate-only state is not silently promoted to VALUE on restart.

\paragraph{Proof boundaries.}
The intended claim is that a surviving RESOLVE decision or resolved binding
preserves its value; if all such copies disappear, any surviving dependent effect
must either provide sufficient recovery evidence or be safely invalidated. This
is a lemma to prove, not a consequence of losing the coordinator alone.
Likewise, replacement of an unrecoverable timestamp must preserve every
IOCL-relevant surviving ordering obligation. A final ACK can externalize an
ordering condition without carrying a timestamp or finalTS. Duplicate suppression
alone does not establish that the reconstructed prefix preserves that condition.

\paragraph{First invariant targets.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item All admissible historical VALUE reports for a binding agree.
\item Every retained historical value is reported; rejected stale messages cannot
introduce an unreported usable binding after a fence, including after restart.
\item Every ongoing reconstruction capable of discovering a binding is represented
by a HOLE; completed NOPE answers cannot silently become incompatible usable data.
\item Holes resolve before incompatible replacements become usable. Accepted values
remain unusable until resolution; late conflicting resolved values are unreachable.
\item Resolution and evidence rediscovery remain safe under arbitrary repeated
leader failovers within the underlying replication protocol's fault assumptions.
\item Reconstructed timestamp floors, READY states, and POQ draining fit the retained
ordered prefix and preserve the ordering obligations exposed by prior final ACKs.
\item Replay, result caching, and duplicate suppression preserve logical operation
identity and client dependency reference counts.
\end{enumerate}
